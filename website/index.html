Visual identity

Sentinel Fold is the primary Wardveil mark.

The standalone Sentinel Fold emblem is the owner-approved primary visual mark for Wardveil Security. Its layered protective veil planes and guarded core communicate defense-in-depth, inspection boundaries, containment, active protection, and controlled visibility. “Wardveil Security” and “Security Center” text are supporting identity and context; they are not part of the emblem itself.

Branding remains separate from security evidence. Displaying Sentinel Fold never proves that a control executed, that malware was scanned, that a runtime is accepted, or that a scope is Protected by Wardveil.

First-party security architecture

Nine capabilities. Explicit authority.

Wardveil Foundation 0.9 combines reusable security services without collapsing decision, execution, evidence, or target-system authority into the interface layer.

Wardveil Trust

Evaluates authoritative identity, session, device, service, and contextual risk signals. Trust remains distinct from authorization.

Wardveil Policy

Produces scoped security decisions from current evidence and policy. A policy decision is not proof that a protection action executed.

Wardveil Protect

Provides the controlled enforcement boundary. High-impact cross-service actions require bound, short-lived runtime authorization plus an explicitly authorized executor.

Wardveil Detect

Represents threat, abuse, behavioral, and anomaly findings while preserving the rule that anomaly alone is not proof of malicious activity.

Wardveil Scan

Normalizes file, content, package, URL, attachment, download, and payload inspection. Unknown or unsupported inspection is never treated as clean.

Quarantine · Response · Audit

Separates isolation from deletion, coordinates bounded incident response, and records security-relevant decisions and outcomes for review and investigation.

Security Center

Presents authoritative Wardveil evidence conservatively for users and administrators. The interface cannot manufacture execution success or protection state.

Durable execution safety

Foundation 0.9 binds high-impact authorization to policy, scope, executor, idempotency, nonce, and validity, then records durable claims and receipts so uncertain outcomes cannot become blind duplicate mutations.

Security principles

Evidence is stronger than branding.

Wardveil is an integral GoreeCloud platform system, not decorative security branding. Its first-party services may hold scoped Wardveil-native authority, but every claim remains bounded by current evidence, explicit contracts, and the authority of the system that owns the represented action or resource.

Authoritative sources remain authoritative

Applications, authentication services, firewalls, infrastructure controls, scanners, and other producers remain responsible for their own technical state until integrated through an explicit Wardveil contract.

Missing evidence fails closed

Stale, unavailable, incomplete, conflicting, expired, unsupported, malformed, or unverified required evidence must never be promoted to a passing state.

Data minimization

Shared evidence must not contain reusable secrets, active credentials, private keys, tokens, recovery material, unrestricted diagnostics, or unnecessary personal data.

Conservative aggregation

A summary cannot be more favorable than the weakest required evidence in its represented scope.

Decision and execution stay distinct

Policy intent, transport success, a Security Center screen, or a durable claim is not proof that an authorized executor completed a protection action.

Scoped protection claims

“Protected by Wardveil” is valid only for the explicit scope backed by current authoritative evidence.

Application conformance

Every GoreeCloud application must remain current with the latest applicable Wardveil Security contract. Missing or unaccepted required integration blocks Stable qualification.

Evidence validity matters

Evidence must remain attributable, current, and within the validity asserted by its producer. A previously passing result cannot justify a current protected state after its evidence expires or is superseded.

Malware protection · Current acceptance boundary

ClamAV is an engine beneath Wardveil Scan—not the Wardveil product boundary.

ClamAV is deployed as Wardveil Scan's initial replaceable signature-based malware engine, with current scanner-health evidence, current signatures, clean-control behavior, EICAR detection, and fail-closed scanner-unavailable behavior. The authenticated Wardveil Scan application transport is also deployed, and durable same-host replay behavior has been runtime-validated across a real service restart. GoreeCloud Drive is runtime-validated against the deployed Scan service for its current bounded upload-enforcement and negative-test scope, and GoreeCloud Mail is runtime-validated for its bounded controlled-provider attachment scope.

Those advances are intentionally narrower than a platform-wide protection claim. The newest Scan hardening source adds stronger transport-negative, temporary credential rotation/revocation, and bounded capacity/recovery acceptance gates, but the latest source still requires exact target-environment redeployment and successful acceptance before it can replace the currently accepted deployed Scan revision. Multi-host replay where applicable, production GoreeCloud Identity service identity and key lifecycle, approved production cryptography and key management, authorized Quarantine execution and readback, authoritative Audit provenance, Security Center provenance, Privacy Shield and Everkeep acceptance, and Stable qualification remain separate gates. Overall production runtime remains unaccepted and protection-claim authority remains false.

Normalized states

Five states with explicit meaning.

ProtectedRequired evidence is current, valid, and supports the protected claim for the stated scope.
AttentionAction or review is needed, but the represented scope is not necessarily degraded.
DegradedA required protection or control is not operating at the expected level.
UnknownRequired evidence is missing, stale, expired, unavailable, incomplete, conflicting, unsupported, or unverified.
Not applicableThe control or evidence does not apply to the represented scope.
Relationship model

One security system. Clear platform boundaries.

Wardveil Security · Security Center

Platform-wide first-party security system and shared security plane for trust, policy, protection, detection, scanning, quarantine, response, audit, security evidence, and protection-state presentation.

Glaze UI · Design Center

Defines how GoreeCloud interfaces look, feel, adapt, and interact across mobile, tablet, desktop, TV, and web surfaces. GLAZE UI V1.1 / 1.1.0 is the current Stable site target; V1.2 Frosted Neutral remains a non-production Candidate.

Privacy Shield · Privacy Center

The separate mandatory platform-wide privacy identity and shared privacy-control foundation. Wardveil integrations preserve Privacy Shield's privacy and minimization authority.

Everkeep · Continuity Center

The separate mandatory resilience, backup and recovery, preservation, portability, succession, continuity, and digital-legacy system. Recovery verification remains an Everkeep authority.

GoreeCloud Mesh · Mesh Center

Coordinates explicit service relationships, bounded evidence exchange, and dependency context without becoming the Wardveil security authority or upgrading security state.

GoreeCloud Identity · Identity Center

Owns identity, authentication, authorization primitives, accounts, devices, credentials, sessions, service identity, and delegated authority. Wardveil consumes only bounded security-relevant identity evidence and does not become the identity authority.

Sentinel Fold, the Wardveil name, a successful deployment, storage health, transport success, or a Security Center view is never evidence by itself that a security control succeeded. Security, privacy, resilience, design, coordination, and identity remain complementary systems with distinct responsibilities.

Presentation semantics

Color can communicate state, but it cannot prove protection.

Wardveil maps normalized security states into Glaze UI semantic color roles for consistent presentation. The mapping is presentation-only and must remain paired with labels, icons, or other non-color cues where meaning matters. A favorable color cannot create or strengthen a protected claim beyond current Wardveil evidence.

Public status principle

Unknown is more trustworthy than invented certainty.

Wardveil deliberately preserves unknown, degraded, and attention states when evidence cannot support a stronger claim. Public protection language should never outrun the underlying implementation or validation evidence.

Portfolio adoption

Mandatory does not mean automatically complete.

The platform-wide mandate requires current Wardveil Security integration in every GoreeCloud application, but the mandate itself does not prove that every repository, artifact, deployment, or runtime has completed adoption. Missing, incomplete, superseded, outdated, unverified, unaccepted, or expired required integration blocks Stable qualification until corrected and revalidated.

Mesh integration

Coordination preserves authority boundaries.

Wardveil publishes minimized security evidence and validity context in forms GoreeCloud Mesh can coordinate across platform relationships. Mesh may transport and relate that context, but it cannot turn missing, expired, degraded, or unknown evidence into a protected state or authorize a Wardveil security effect merely by delivering a message.
