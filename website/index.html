It coordinates evidence-backed trust, policy, protection, detection, scanning, quarantine, response, audit, and security presentation while preserving the authority of the systems and executors that own underlying technical actions.

Visual identity

Sentinel Fold is the primary Wardveil mark.

The approved Sentinel Fold emblem is synchronized from GoreeCloud’s canonical branding authority. Its layered protective veil planes and guarded core communicate defense-in-depth, inspection boundaries, containment, active protection, and controlled visibility.

Branding remains separate from security evidence. Displaying Sentinel Fold never proves that a control executed, malware was scanned, or a scope is protected.

Malware protection boundary

ClamAV is an engine beneath Wardveil Scan.

ClamAV is an initial replaceable signature-based malware engine used by supported Wardveil Scan paths. Scanner health, source integration, a successful build, or a public website never establishes broad platform-wide protection by itself.

Production security, identity, scanning, quarantine, audit, privacy, recovery, and application-specific acceptance remain evidence-gated. Where required evidence is missing, stale, unsupported, conflicting, or unverified, Wardveil must preserve a non-passing state.

Normalized states

Five states with explicit meaning.

ProtectedCurrent authoritative evidence supports the protected claim for the stated scope.
AttentionAction or review is needed without automatically implying degradation.
DegradedA required protection or control is below its expected state.
UnknownRequired evidence is missing, stale, unavailable, incomplete, conflicting, unsupported, or unverified.
Not applicableThe control or evidence does not apply to the represented scope.

Presentation semantics

Color communicates state; it does not prove protection.

Wardveil maps normalized security states into consistent presentation roles while keeping labels and other non-color cues. Favorable visual treatment cannot create or strengthen a protected claim beyond current authoritative evidence.
